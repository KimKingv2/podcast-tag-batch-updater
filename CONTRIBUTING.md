# Contributing

Bug reports and small improvements are welcome. When reporting a problem, include:

- Windows/Python version and the audio container (MP3, M4A, FLAC, etc.).
- A redacted `podcast.json` example and relevant log lines.
- Steps to reproduce the issue.

Please do not upload copyrighted podcast audio, personal media libraries, private RSS URLs, or personal metadata to public issues. Use small synthetic test files whenever possible.
